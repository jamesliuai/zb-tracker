# The PLL algorithms. Run tools/build_pll.py after changing anything here.
#
#   yiheng   Yiheng Wang's, the first one being the main algorithm. A fingertrick can follow a |
#   two      other two-handed algorithms, and two+ for the ones behind "more"
#   oh       one-handed algorithms, and oh+ for the ones behind "more"
#
# The first algorithm of a case decides which way its picture faces, so that it needs no AUF.
#
# Where they come from
#   Yiheng Wang's are from the description of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSOp5nAudys by CubeMaster,
#   and the fingertricks are from what is said in it.
#   Gb and Y follow the comment by @Zephorex there: by 2025 Yiheng had switched to the RUD algorithm for Gb,
#   which the video still has second, and does Y from home grip.
#   The rest are from https://speedcube.quest/algorithms/3x3/pll, most used in real solves first.
#
# What was changed
#   Gb was written with R2' U D at the end, and the third H with a U at the end. That U is the final AUF, so it's gone.
#   The second Ua had a dot in the middle of it, for a regrip.
#   Algorithms from SpeedcubeQuest lost the rotations and U turns around them that only set the AUF.
